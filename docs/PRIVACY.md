# Privacy policy (DRAFT TEMPLATE - have it reviewed before publishing)
**What we collect:** account email and a password hash (only if you sign in with OAuth); product searches and the region you enter; your IP address for rate limiting (kept 24 hours). If you tap "Use my location", your coordinates are rounded to about 1 km and sent to OpenStreetMap to find your city; we do not store them.
**Who processes it:** Anthropic (to run searches and read store pages), OpenStreetMap (location lookup), our hosting provider.
**Retention:** search jobs 7 days; cached results 30 minutes; price history 180 days (no personal data); accounts until you delete them (account deletion endpoint) or ask us to.
**Your rights:** contact [YOUR EMAIL] to access or delete your data.
**Prices** are informational and may be wrong or outdated; verify with the store.
